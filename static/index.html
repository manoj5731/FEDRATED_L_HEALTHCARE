Strategy Breakdown

FedAvg Federated Averaging

Standard benchmark. Coordinates clients and computes parameter-wise weighted averages after local SGD epochs.

FedProx Proximal Term Regularization

Adds μ/2 ||w - wt||2 loss penalty. Stabilizes convergence in heterogeneous and non-IID distributions.

FedAdam Server-Side Adaptive Optimization

Maintains first and second momentum moments on client pseudo-gradients for faster initial acceleration.
